Product Sheet: AstroLamp (Adjustable Desk Lamp)

Summary
AstroLamp is a compact desk lamp with adjustable brightness and color temperature. It’s designed for late-night work without harsh glare.

Controls
- Tap: toggle power
- Press and hold: adjust brightness (1% to 100%)
- Double-tap: cycle color temperature

Color temperature modes
- Warm: 2700K (relaxing, evening)
- Neutral: 4000K (general work)
- Cool: 6500K (detail work, daytime)

Tips
- For reading at night, use Warm or Neutral with 30% to 60% brightness.
- If you feel eye strain, lower brightness and reduce blue light (Warm mode).
