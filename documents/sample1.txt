Galactic Gadgets: Company Overview

Galactic Gadgets is a fictional e-commerce shop that sells space-themed consumer electronics and accessories.

Testing note
- If you run a search for the word "test", this overview document should be a strong match.

What we sell
- StarScope Mini: compact telescope attachment for phones
- NebulaNoise ANC Headphones: active noise-cancelling headphones
- OrbitCharge Pro: 3-in-1 wireless charging dock (phone, earbuds, watch)
- AstroLamp: desk lamp with adjustable color temperature (2700K to 6500K)

Support expectations
- Typical response time: within 1 business day
- Order issues: include order ID and the email used at checkout
- Warranty: 12 months for most electronics; consumables excluded

Store policies (high-level)
- Returns: allowed within 30 days for unused items in original packaging
- Exchanges: allowed for defective items within warranty
