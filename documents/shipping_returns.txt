Galactic Gadgets: Shipping, Returns, and Warranty

Shipping
- Processing time: 1 to 2 business days
- Standard shipping: 3 to 7 business days (domestic)
- Expedited shipping: 1 to 3 business days (domestic)
- International: timing varies by destination and customs

Tracking
- Tracking is emailed after the label is created.
- Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement.

Returns
- Return window: 30 days from delivery
- Condition: unused items in original packaging
- Return shipping: customer-paid unless the item is defective or incorrect

Exchanges
- For defects within 12 months, we replace the item (same model) when available.
- If the model is discontinued, we provide an equivalent replacement.

Warranty
- Electronics: 12-month limited warranty
- Exclusions: accidental damage, water damage (unless explicitly rated), consumables

Support checklist
- Provide: order ID, email used at checkout, and a brief description of the issue
- For charging issues: include the power adapter wattage and cable type
