Product Sheet: NebulaNoise ANC Headphones

Summary
NebulaNoise ANC Headphones are over-ear headphones designed for travel and focused work. They support active noise cancellation (ANC), transparency mode, and multipoint Bluetooth.

Key features
- ANC modes: Off, On, Adaptive
- Transparency mode: lets outside sound in for announcements
- Bluetooth: 5.3 with multipoint (two devices)
- Battery life: up to 32 hours (ANC On), up to 40 hours (ANC Off)
- Charging: USB-C, 10 minutes quick charge for ~4 hours playback

Pairing steps
1) Hold the power button for 3 seconds to enter pairing mode.
2) On your phone/laptop, open Bluetooth settings and select “NebulaNoise”.
3) If multipoint is enabled, repeat for the second device.

Troubleshooting
- Audio stutters: move closer to the source and disable multipoint temporarily.
- Mic sounds quiet: ensure the boom mic is fully extended (if attached).
