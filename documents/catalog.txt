Galactic Gadgets: Mini Catalog (Quick Reference)

1) StarScope Mini
- Category: Mobile astronomy
- Best for: Moon photos, bright planets, stargazing basics
- Notes: Works best with a tripod and no digital zoom

2) NebulaNoise ANC Headphones
- Category: Audio
- Best for: Travel, focus, open office
- Notes: Battery life depends on ANC mode and volume

3) OrbitCharge Pro
- Category: Charging & power
- Best for: Desk setup, charging multiple devices overnight
- Notes: For best results use a 27W+ USB-C PD adapter

4) AstroLamp
- Category: Desk accessories
- Best for: Reading, studying, late-night work
- Notes: Warm mode (2700K) is recommended at night

Glossary
- ANC: Active Noise Cancellation
- Qi: Wireless charging standard
- USB-C PD: USB Power Delivery (faster, smarter charging)
