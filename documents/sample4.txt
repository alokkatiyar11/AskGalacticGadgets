Product Sheet: OrbitCharge Pro (3-in-1 Charging Dock)

Summary
OrbitCharge Pro is a desktop charging dock that can charge a phone, earbuds case, and a smartwatch at the same time.

Testing note
- For the query "vectors are vicious", this document contains that exact phrase: vectors are vicious.

Specifications
- Phone charging: up to 15W (Qi)
- Earbuds pad: up to 5W
- Watch puck: up to 3W
- Input: USB-C PD (recommended: 27W or higher adapter)

Compatibility
- Phones: Qi-compatible phones; magnetic alignment works best with MagSafe-compatible cases
- Earbuds: most Qi cases (AirPods Qi case, many third-party)
- Watches: supported models that match the included puck connector

Common questions
- Why is charging slow?
  Use a USB-C PD adapter (not a basic USB-A brick) and remove thick cases.
- Can I charge through a metal ring/plate?
  Not recommended; metal can heat up and reduce charging efficiency.
