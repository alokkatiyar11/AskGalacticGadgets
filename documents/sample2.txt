Product Sheet: StarScope Mini

Summary
StarScope Mini is a compact telescope attachment designed for modern smartphones. It clips onto the phone camera and provides optical magnification for stargazing and moon photography.

Testing note
- Integration tests may query "How about Python?" (Python) and expect this file to be returned.

Key specifications
- Magnification: 8x optical
- Field of view: 12 degrees
- Lens coating: multi-coated (reduces glare)
- Compatibility: most phones 65mm to 85mm wide
- Mount type: adjustable clip with soft pads

Setup steps
1) Clean the phone camera lens.
2) Attach StarScope Mini to the phone so the lens is centered.
3) Open your camera app and zoom to 1.0x (avoid digital zoom).
4) Focus by turning the focus ring until stars or the moon look sharp.

Common issues
- Blurry image: the lens is likely not centered; re-clip and refocus.
- Dark corners (vignetting): reduce digital zoom and re-center.
- Shaky shots: use a small tripod and a 3-second timer.
